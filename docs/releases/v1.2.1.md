Fix release of the recipe: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 (EXL3 2.9 bpw, Engram from local NVMe) on two DGX Sparks at TP=2 over CX-7, with sparkrun.

Image: `ghcr.io/ursuciprian/ds41-tensorfold:v1.2.1`, digest `sha256:d6511e9187edbf8facc632d8a475f457be91f01f611210e34c75329909d6b3b9` (engine commit `e5574f9`, v1.2.0 plus one fix; the gated build with image labels added, same layers).

Drafter: D1, unchanged (`ursuciprian/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-DSpark-D1` @ `ca1a99a8f07e4ec45addc71d77f5fc73ee3b1c5f`).

What is in it:
- a fix for a CUDA graph capture crash under torch 2.13's automatic garbage collection. torch 2.13 no longer collects before a capture, so a collection could start inside one, free an old graph and invalidate the capture ("operation failed due to a previous error during capture"); the engine then turned graphs off and ran eager kernels. Captures now run with the cyclic collector paused, and the caller's collector state comes back after each capture, also after a failed one. v1.2.0 has the bug.

No speed claims: the fix changes no kernel and no setting.

Quality, on the gated build with this recipe and D1 on 2026-11-06: the full gate passed, exactness 32 of 32 at C1 and C8, 27 of 27 greedy replies against v1.0.1, and needle retrieval exact on 20 of 20 at 247K in the gate and on two more runs at 241K. Engine tests: 401 passed, 1 failed; the one failure (`test_spec_drafted_equals_serial_gpu[4]`) also fails on v1.2.0.

Upgrading from v1.2.0: pull the new image; the recipe changes only in the image tag. Every setting stays as in v1.2.0.

Setup: docs/setup.md. Known limits and the shared-server note: as in v1.2.0 (#11, #13).

Release of the recipe: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 (EXL3 2.9 bpw, Engram from local NVMe) on two DGX Sparks at TP=2 over CX-7, with sparkrun.

Image: `ghcr.io/ursuciprian/ds41-tensorfold:v1.2.2`, digest `sha256:c0eb8c0d12df8ce70856ae6d4dd9f830e8cc89ec52c26251cbdcba8bed73a806` (engine commit `bd99990`, v1.2.1 plus prefill logging and one request fix; the gated build with image labels added, same layers).

Drafter: D1, unchanged (`ursuciprian/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-DSpark-D1` @ `ca1a99a8f07e4ec45addc71d77f5fc73ee3b1c5f`).

What is in it:
- `NCCL_MAX_CTAS: '4'` in the recipe. NCCL then pins 122 MiB of host memory per rank instead of 1,225 MiB, and the engine uses the freed 1.1 GiB to fill long prompts in 2,048-row pieces instead of 1,024. Prefill stays exact; only NCCL's channel count changes.
- The server honours a top-level `"thinking": {"type": "disabled"}` (or `"enabled"`), as DeepSeek-style clients send it. v1.2.1 ignored the field and kept reasoning on. `chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking` still wins when both are set.
- Prefill logging: `[dsv41 pfrows]` lines give each prompt's piece sizes, and `TF_DSV41_MEMPROBE_LOW=N` logs free memory at a round's peak every N seconds. Off unless set; the recipe does not set it.

Measured on my pair, v1.2.2 settings against v1.2.1 settings in alternating boots (A B B A) on 2026-11-09, one image, the NCCL line the only difference, under a keep rule written before the first boot:
- agentic C8, 24 prompts per bucket: wall time -18.8% / -21.7% at 8K, -26.1% / -25.8% at 16K, -23.2% / -19.7% at 32K (two boot pairs); median time to first token -40% to -57%; replies text-identical on 144 of 144.
- eight 124K-token prompts at once: 467 and 465 s against 642 and 695 s, 8 of 8 answered on every boot; lowest MemAvailable on rank 0 7.2 and 7.3 GiB against 6.3 and 6.0 GiB.
- one prompt at a time: every 32K and 128K prompt ran at 2,048 rows, 2,000 to 2,066 tok/s at 32K and 2,084 to 2,152 at 128K. On v1.2.1 settings one boot ran 5 of 6 such prompts at 1,024 rows (1,603 to 1,806 tok/s) and the other all at 2,048.
- decode: no cell worse beyond noise; I do not claim a decode gain.

Quality, on the release build with this recipe and D1 on 2026-11-10 (one boot, healthy after 280 s): the full gate passed (hardmode 93, bar 88), exactness 32 of 32 at C1 and C8, 27 of 27 greedy replies against v1.0.1, and needle retrieval exact on 20 of 20 at 247K in the gate and on two more runs at 241K. Thinking switch: top-level `disabled` gave 0 reasoning characters, `enabled` and no field 57, same answer. Engine tests: 407 passed, 19 skipped, 1 failed; the one failure (`test_spec_drafted_equals_serial_gpu[4]`) also fails on v1.2.1. Five mutants of the new code were caught by the tests.

Upgrading from v1.2.1: pull the new image and add `NCCL_MAX_CTAS: '4'` to the recipe's `env`, or take the new recipe. Every other setting stays as in v1.2.1.
